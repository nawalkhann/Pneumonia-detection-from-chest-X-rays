How to read these numbers

Why the threshold is 0.34

A classifier outputs a probability, and the threshold turns it into a yes or no. The threshold was picked on the validation set only, as the highest cut-off that keeps validation recall at or above 98%. It came out at 0.34. On the test set it gives the same 99.0% recall as 0.50, because the four missed cases all score below 0.30, and it adds a few more false alarms. The recall article explains this choice in more detail.

Why specificity drops on the test set

On validation data, specificity was 99.3%. On the official test set it is 62.8%. This gap is a known property of this dataset: the test split was collected differently from the training images, and most published models on it show the same drop. These are the honest test-set numbers, not validation numbers.

Validation results

On the 573-image patient-level validation split, the model reached a ROC-AUC of 0.999, recall of 98.8%, specificity of 99.3% and precision of 99.8% at the 0.34 threshold.
